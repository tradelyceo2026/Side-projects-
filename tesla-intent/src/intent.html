1 · Grounded capability

Every action the assistant can take is a registered capability with a verified flag. It cannot describe an action it has no entry for. Ask it to roll down a window and watch what happens.

2 · Consent before actuation

Comfort changes apply immediately. Anything that spends money, moves the car, or touches security becomes a proposal card you confirm with one tap. The assistant never silently commits those.

DIRECT climate, seats, volume, lock
PROPOSE charging, navigation, sentry, schedules

3 · Standing intents

A command is one-shot. An intent persists and keeps being true. "Never let me leave for a job under 40 percent" is a rule the car re-evaluates on every state change, not a thing you ask for each morning.

4 · Model routing

Three brains, one surface. On-device handles safety-critical and latency-bound work with no network. Conversational and live-data queries go to the resident assistant. Long-horizon planning, documents and structured reasoning route out to a reasoning model.

ON-DEVICE RESIDENT REASONING

Independent design concept. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by Tesla, Inc. Vehicle state is simulated; the capability names mirror the shape of the public Tesla Fleet API so the prototype maps cleanly onto a real integration.
